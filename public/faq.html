Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

Ordering & Payment

Simply browse our products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll fill in your shipping details and choose a payment method. No account is required — guest checkout is always available.

We currently accept Bank Transfer and PayPal. For bank transfers, your order will be processed after the payment is confirmed. PayPal payments are secure and processed immediately.

If your order hasn't been processed yet, we may be able to modify or cancel it. Please contact us as soon as possible with your order number, and we'll do our best to help.

You can track your order anytime using our Order Tracking page. Simply enter the order number you received in your confirmation email. The format looks like this: CB-2026000125.

Shipping & Delivery

Shipping times vary depending on your location. Typically, orders are processed within 1-2 business days, and delivery takes 3-7 business days for domestic orders. International shipping may take 7-14 business days.

We offer free shipping on orders that meet a certain threshold. Check our store settings or the checkout page for the current free shipping minimum. This threshold is displayed during checkout.

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. During checkout, you can enter your country and city. International shipping costs and delivery times vary by destination.

Products & Warranty

All our products come with a manufacturer's warranty. The warranty period varies by product and covers manufacturing defects. Please check the product description for specific warranty details or contact us for more information.

Each product comes with specific cleaning instructions in the user manual. Generally, we recommend cleaning your humidifier or diffuser weekly with mild soap and water, and descaling monthly with white vinegar to prevent mineral buildup. Always unplug before cleaning.

Most of our aroma diffusers are compatible with standard essential oils. We recommend using 100% pure essential oils for the best experience and to avoid damaging the diffuser. Always follow the manufacturer's guidelines for oil-to-water ratios.

Returns & Refunds

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for most products. Items must be in original condition with all accessories and packaging. Please see our Refund Policy page for complete details.

Once we receive and inspect your return, refunds are typically processed within 5-10 business days. The refund will be issued to your original payment method. Bank transfers may take additional processing time depending on your bank.

If the return is due to a defective product or our error, we will cover the return shipping cost. For all other returns, the customer is responsible for return shipping. Please contact us before returning any item.

Still have questions?

We're here to help. Reach out to our support team and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
